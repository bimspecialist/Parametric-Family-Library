Playable demonstration / تجربة الألعاب

Click, touch, move a pointer or use arrow keys and Enter. This is a local browser demo, not physical body tracking. Two teams share the same screen. No camera, microphone or personal data collection.

The projection, LED modules and sensor housings are conceptual. Physical tracking hardware, display specification, anchorage, electrical design, access and safe play clearances require separate review. Hex modules use flat backing; curved displays use faceted segments.

Revit workflow

Export OBJ, MTL and PNG into the same folder. Geometry is static; screen texture may require manual material assignment. Complete wall-mounted export retains the rear mounting plane Y=0 and floor datum Z=0. Horizontal gaming floor exports rest at Z=0, with their display surface facing +Z. Floor assembly thickness is measured from the bottom to the playing surface. Individual pieces are centered in XY at their lowest Z. Import at millimetre scale in a Generic Model or face-based family template and save as RFA. HTML parameters are not native RFA parameters.
