Ball movement is an illustrative path animation, not a verified magnetic or mechanical simulation. Exported geometry is static. Actual magnet selection, containment, field strength, track clearances, structural supports and electrical safety require professional design and review.

Revit workflow

Choose a complete installation, a group, or any numbered individual component. Export OBJ and MTL to the same folder. Individual parts are centered in XY with their base at Z = 0. For both wall mounting modes, the complete export retains the wall plane Y = 0 and floor datum Z = 0; use a face-based family template if wall hosting is required, and verify insertion/orientation manually. Use a Metric Generic Model or Furniture family template, import OBJ with millimetres and check scale/materials, then save as RFA. For offline use, the generator needs no external code; the logo/navigation assets are optional.
