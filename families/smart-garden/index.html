Wall mounting & Revit workflow

Wall-mounted mode includes rear rails and brackets, no floor legs. A cabinet may be omitted for a floating shelf system with a remote reservoir. Floor-supported mode rests on its bottom frame; rear restraint rails are optional. The flat rear datum is Y=0; Z=0 is the lowest edge, so wall-mount floor clearance is a placement offset, not exported geometry. The preview wall and floor are not exported. Export OBJ and MTL in the same folder. In Revit 2027: New Family → Metric Generic Model → Insert → Import CAD → OBJ → check mm and assign materials → Save RFA → Load into Project. Use elevation offset to set floor clearance.

Concept geometry only: the cabinet represents an enclosed reservoir, not a detailed tank or pump. Structural anchors, water loads, leakage protection, drains, electrical protection, lighting and maintenance access require specialist coordination. NFT channels and tray alternatives are schematic, not fabrication details.
