Revit workflow

Export OBJ, MTL and PNG into the same folder. Individual components are centered at their base; wall-mounted full station retains Y=0 rear datum. Import geometry at millimetre scale into a Generic Model or face-based family and save as RFA. Glass, emissive effects and screen materials may need manual assignment. Optical technology, anchorage, electrical safety and accessibility require separate specification.
